(b)

Childhood

The childhood period ranges from the age of 2 years to 13 years. During this period, a child increases in height and weight, hence, a lot of changes in the body occur. During this stage the milk teeth are replaced by permanent teeth. The child develops skills in climbing, jumping, riding, running, self-feeding, drawing and colouring. The child learns social roles like dressing, eating and bathing on their own. During this period, the development of a child’s skills with the help of parents or guardians is very important. Interactions among children themselves and their parents or adults are also important. Children need to be provided with a balanced diet, a conducive environment for playing, proper health services, regular cleanliness, shelter and clothing.

(c)

Adolescence

Adolescence is a transition stage between childhood and adulthood. It is during this stage that puberty occurs. Puberty is defined as the phase where a child’s physical and sexual characteristics start to mature into adult features. A child also undergoes physical, mental and emotional changes which are driven by hormones. Puberty usually starts between the ages of 12 to 13 years in boys and 11 to 12 years in girls, though it can vary due to genetic, nutritional and environmental factors.
