Avoiding alcohol, tobacco, and other substances: These substances affect the nervous system and impair decision-making abilities. Since puberty comes with challenges in making the right choices, avoiding substances and maintaining a healthy lifestyle is crucial. Respecting others also promotes good morals.

Sharing challenges with friends or family: Every adolescent experiences puberty differently. Sometimes, young people think they are the only ones facing these changes, leading to emotional distress, anxiety, and low self-esteem. Talking to others helps in getting the right guidance and support.

Seeking help from trusted individuals: Adolescents may face difficulties they cannot handle alone. It is important to seek advice from trusted people such as parents, guardians, teachers and other reliable adults.

Maintaining personal hygiene: Personal hygiene is crucial during puberty. This includes bathing regularly, washing clothes, ironing and keeping hair neat. For girls, changing sanitary pads or menstrual products in time during menstruation is essential for hygiene and comfort.

(d) Adulthood

Adulthood is a stage where physical growth slows down or stops, and the body focuses more on maintaining overall health rather than increasing in size. During this period, the rate of growth is minimal. This occurs in the age range of 18 - 65 years. This is the period when human beings mature with full physical and intellectual maturity. A person in this stage is self-confident and is more likely to make sound decisions and take responsibility. An adult person requires proper nutrition and should engage in regular physical exercises.

(e) Old age (Elderliness)

Old age occurs normally at the age of 65 years and above. This is the period when the body systems and muscles become weak in comparison to those in the younger stages. Physical body strength decreases, body hair becomes grey and vision and hearing ability decrease. Skin loses elasticity and a person experiences loss of memory. Elderly people require maintaining personal hygiene, eating a balanced diet and taking light physical exercises. Also, doing regular health checkups is important to maintain a good health status.
