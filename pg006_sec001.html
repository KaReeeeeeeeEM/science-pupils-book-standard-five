Introduction

The Science Pupil’s Textbook Standard Five has been prepared in line with the 2023 Science Syllabus for Primary Schools Standard III–VI, issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. Some content in this book has been adapted from Science and Technology for Standards Five and Six textbooks, published in 2018 in accordance with the 2016 curriculum and syllabus.

This textbook has five chapters, namely; Digestive system, Growth and development in plants and animals, Reproductive system, Magnetism and Computer coding. The content in this book has been presented through text, activities, experiments, exercises and illustrations. This has been done to enable you to develop the required competencies in each chapter. Each chapter has exercises to enable you assess your understanding of the respective content. Thus, you are required to do all activities, experiments, questions and exercises on your own or in collaboration with your peers. Moreover, consult your teachers, parents or guardians when searching for information from the library, the internet and other sources.

Additional learning resources are available in TIE e-Library at https://ol.tie.go.tz or ol.tie.go.tz
