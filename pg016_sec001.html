Vocabulary

Absorption

the process by which digested food molecules pass into the blood or lymph

Chyme

semi-fluid mixture of partially digested food, water, stomach acid (hydrochloric acid) and digestive enzymes formed in the stomach, which passes into small intestine for further digestion and nutrient absorption

Digestive Enzymes

chemicals that aid in the digestion of food in the digestive system

Gland

an organ in the human or animal body that secretes hormones for specific body functions

Nutrients

substances in food that provide energy and materials for growth, repair, and maintenance
