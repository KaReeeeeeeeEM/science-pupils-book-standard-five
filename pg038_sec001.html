Chapter Three

Reproductive system

Introduction

Reproduction is one of the characteristics of living organisms. Organisms reproduce to increase their population size. In this chapter, you will learn about the concept of the human reproductive system, the parts that make up the human reproductive system and their functions. Additionally, you will learn about some disorders in the human reproductive system. The competencies developed will enable you to care for your reproductive system and avoid challenges that may affect it.

The concept of the reproductive system

Activity 1

Search for information about the meaning and importance of the reproductive system from the library or reliable online resources.

Reproduction is a biological process by which living organisms give rise to offspring. Reproduction ensures an increase in number of living things, and continuity of the species from one generation to another. There are two types of reproduction, namely, sexual and asexual reproduction. Sexual reproduction involves the fusion of reproductive cells (sperm and egg) to form an offspring. In humans and some plants, the reproductive cells are produced by two parents (male and female parents).
