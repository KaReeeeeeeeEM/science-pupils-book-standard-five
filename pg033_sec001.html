• (b)

    Desire for recognition: Adolescents at this stage seek recognition for their roles in society and the world. This desire is influenced by gender, peer groups, culture and family expectations.

  • (c)

    Desire for freedom: At this age, adolescents want to make their own decisions without interference from parents or guardians. This often leads to conflicts between them and their parents or guardians. While parents have a responsibility to guide their children, adolescents must also recognise the role of their parents and practise responsible freedom.

  • (d)

    Curiosity to learn new things: During puberty, boys and girls become curious and eager to try new experiences. This curiosity can sometimes lead to risky behaviours. For example, they may want to learn skills such as driving cars and riding motorcycles. Some may start smoking or abusing drugs. This urge can also lead to early sexual activity, which may result in unintended pregnancies or sexually transmitted infections like HIV.

Mental changes during puberty

During puberty, boys and girls experience mental changes that influence their behaviour. These changes include:

  • (a)

    Increased ability to understand reality in their surroundings.

  • (b)

    Improved problem-solving skills, even for complex and confusing issues.

  • (c)

    Enhanced ability to understand other people’s thoughts and perspectives.

  • (d)

    Ignoring the consequences of some of their actions.

Changes in relationships during puberty

During puberty, boys and girls also show changes in their interactions with society, such as:

  • (a)

    Spending more time with friends than family: At this stage, adolescents feel that friends are more important than family. They believe that, peers understand them better because they share similar thoughts. As a result, they prefer spending more time with friends instead of family.

  • (b)

    Developing different opinions from adults: Adolescents start forming their own opinions and questioning what they see or hear.
