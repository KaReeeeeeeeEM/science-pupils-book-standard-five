Table 2 continued: Parts of the female reproductive system and their functions
No. Structure Description Function(s)
5 Vagina The vagina is part of the female reproductive system located between the cervix and the external parts of the body.
  • To receive sperm.
  • It serves as the passage for the baby during childbirth.
  • It provides a route for menstrual blood to pass out of the body.
  • It protects other reproductive organs from infections by maintaining the acidic environment that prevents harmful pathogens.
