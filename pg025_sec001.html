Growth in animals

Activity 2

Listen to or watch an online video that explains growth in animals. Then, write a brief description of animal growth.

Growth and development in animals depends on the availability of the factors such as weather and climatic conditions, nutrition and availability of water.

Nutrition

Animals require a balanced diet containing all essential nutrients. Lack of nutrients, such as proteins, carbohydrates, fats, vitamins, or minerals in their food can result in poor growth and development.

Environmental condition

Animals are affected by their surroundings. Unfavourable environmental conditions such as heat, cold, or poor air quality and space can stress animals and reduce their growth rate. Animals need optimum temperature
